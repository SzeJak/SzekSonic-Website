The craft

Every piece begins as chopped carbon tow, laid into the mould by hand and pressed under heat. The fibres flow where the mould takes them, then the piece is trimmed, wet-sanded, and polished until the marbling reads like stone under glass.

Gloss or satin finish · every pattern one of a kind · custom shapes and one-off commissions welcome.
