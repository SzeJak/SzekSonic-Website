Craft

Pressed, not woven.

Chopped tow, not cloth

Forged carbon starts as short strands of carbon fibre laid into the mould by hand, not a woven sheet.

Pressed under heat

As the mould closes the fibres flow into every edge. That flow is what leaves the marbling.

Finished by hand

Each piece is trimmed, wet-sanded and polished in our shop in the Czech Republic.

Every surface is one of one.
